01 / THE LABEL

Built on independence.
Driven by sound.

ELFACYEL MUSIC is an independent record label and music company from Algeria, created to support artists while respecting their creative identity and rights.

From music production and artist development to release strategy and promotion, our focus is simple: build meaningful music projects with a long-term vision.
